      <p>Pafford BI LLC is a Microsoft BI, Fabric, and analytics architecture practice. The work is to turn fragmented reporting environments into governed analytics systems that leaders can trust, scale, and actually use.</p>
      <p>The practice is built around architecture, not dashboard volume: semantic models, governance, lifecycle, reliability, and decision-ready reporting on the Microsoft stack.</p>
    </article>

    <article class="page-card">
      <h2>How the work is approached</h2>
      <ul class="include-list">
        <li>Diagnose before building. The first useful deliverable is clarity.</li>
        <li>Prefer a bounded improvement that the client can operate over an open-ended build.</li>
        <li>Treat Fabric, capacity, and new platform features as means, not as the recommendation by default.</li>
        <li>Keep public material client-safe. Portfolio proof should show method and architecture, not other people's data.</li>
