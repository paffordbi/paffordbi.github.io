    <article class="page-card">
      <h2>BI Architecture Assessment</h2>
      <p>Understand what is broken, why it matters, and what to fix first.</p>
      <p>The Assessment is a tightly capped discovery engagement. It produces a decision-ready view of the current estate and a short list of changes that are worth making.</p>
      <p><strong>Typically includes</strong></p>
      <ul class="include-list">
        <li>Stakeholder discovery and an inventory of the relevant reporting estate</li>
        <li>Review of selected semantic models, reports, workspaces, and security patterns</li>
        <li>Review of refresh and pipeline reliability</li>
        <li>Observations on KPI definitions, ownership, and governance</li>
        <li>A target-state recommendation, a prioritized backlog, and a 30/60/90-day roadmap</li>
      </ul>
      <p><strong>It does not include</strong></p>
      <ul class="exclude-list">
        <li>Full remediation or a complete rebuild</li>
        <li>A penetration test, security audit, or legal or compliance certification</li>
        <li>An exhaustive review of every report</li>
        <li>24x7 production support, licenses, or cloud consumption</li>
      </ul>
      <p>The readout ends with a clear recommendation: take no action, start a bounded Modernization Sprint, or engage Fractional architecture support.</p>
    </article>

    <article class="page-card">
      <h2>Modernization Sprint</h2>
      <p>Implement one bounded, high-value architecture or analytics improvement.</p>
      <p>A sprint starts from a named objective and named acceptance criteria. Typical examples include a semantic-model redesign, workspace and governance setup, pipeline modernization, a governed KPI layer, or an executive reporting pattern.</p>
      <p><strong>Typically includes</strong></p>
      <ul class="include-list">
        <li>One agreed modernization objective</li>
        <li>A bounded set of sources, models, and reports</li>
        <li>Implementation, validation, and a short stabilization window</li>
        <li>Documentation and a handoff the internal team can operate</li>
      </ul>
      <p><strong>It does not include</strong></p>
      <ul class="exclude-list">
        <li>New business domains or data sources beyond the agreed scope</li>
        <li>A major ERP migration</li>
        <li>Unlimited report pages</li>
        <li>Ongoing production support after the agreed stabilization window</li>
      </ul>
      <p>If the work needs to grow, that is a change in scope, not an informal extension.</p>
    </article>

    <article class="page-card">
      <h2>Fractional BI Architect</h2>
      <p>Ongoing senior architecture leadership without a full-time hire.</p>
      <p>This is retained architecture capacity: a visible backlog, a regular working session, written architecture decisions, and reusable standards. It is not a ticket queue and not staff augmentation.</p>
      <p><strong>Typically includes</strong></p>
      <ul class="include-list">
        <li>Architecture review and roadmap prioritization</li>
        <li>Standards, governance, and model or pipeline design</li>
        <li>Coaching of internal analysts or developers</li>
        <li>A defined monthly capacity band, agreed in advance</li>
      </ul>
      <p><strong>It does not include</strong></p>
      <ul class="exclude-list">
        <li>Guaranteed after-hours response</li>
        <li>Unlimited unused-hour rollover, unless a later agreement says otherwise</li>
        <li>Client license costs</li>
        <li>Work that requires access or authority the client has not provisioned</li>
      </ul>
      <p>Current retainer capacity is limited. Larger, near-full-time delivery retainers are not offered at this stage.</p>
    </article>

    <article class="page-card">
      <h2>How the work usually proceeds</h2>
      <ol class="steps">
        <li>A short qualification conversation to confirm the pain, the buyer, and access.</li>
        <li>A paid Assessment, if the problem is a fit.</li>
        <li>An executive readout with a recommended next step — including the option to stop.</li>
        <li>A Modernization Sprint only when the objective is bounded and the acceptance criteria are clear.</li>
        <li>Fractional support only when ongoing architecture leverage is the right shape, not when the need is a single project.</li>
